# Rebound Arena

A self-contained Godot 4.7 top-down 3D arena survival game. The arena, Shinchan-inspired low-poly player, enemies, Goro Goro Chips health packs, effects, and procedural audio are generated by the project scripts; no imported assets are required.

## Controls

- **WASD**: move
- **Mouse**: aim
- **Left click** or on-screen **FIRE** button: fire (the button auto-aims at the nearest enemy)
- **Space**: dash (1.2-second cooldown)
- **Play Again** button: restart after the run ends

Survive for five minutes to clear the arena. Enemy waves grow over time and are capped at twelve active enemies. Taking damage reduces health and grants the next upgrade, with a short invulnerability window to prevent repeated hits from the same burst. After hit five, every third hit grants a 5% damage bonus. Goro Goro Chips packs are collected automatically when Shinchan walks close and restore up to 35 health, capped at 100. Shinchan carries a visible energy blaster that flashes when firing.

## Upgrades

1. Fire rate +5%
2. Move speed +10%
3. Bullet damage +15%
4. Fire rate +20%
5. Explosive bullets
6. Every third hit after that: damage +5%

The game includes a quiet looping tension drone, generated shooting/dash/hit/pickup/end cues, a health bar, timer, hit and upgrade counts, dash status, defeat count, and complete end-of-run stats. The character has a simple bob/run animation, hit flash, and dash squash; enemies pulse, bullets flash, and explosions expand and fade.
